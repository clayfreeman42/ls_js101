# "Precon Manager" and Tools for Hospital Layout / Nurse-Travel Optimization

Research note (as of Nov 2026). Several vendor domains (beck-technology.com, concntric.com, autodesk.com, healthcaredesignmagazine.com) could not be fetched directly from this research session (network allowlist). Claims about them below come from search-result snippets and third-party listings, not from full-page reads, so treat feature details as lower-confidence.

## What exactly is "Precon Manager"? Are there multiple products with that name? Who sells it?

### Takeaway
No standalone commercial product named exactly "Precon Manager" (or "PreconManager") could be found. The phrase shows up mainly as a buyer-persona or role label on preconstruction vendors' marketing pages, most clearly Beck Technology's "For Preconstruction Managers & VPs" page at /precon-manager, which promotes its DESTINI platform, and ConCntric's "For Precon and Design Managers" page. Several similarly named or similar-purpose platforms exist, including PreconSuite, Precon Playbook, ConCntric, DESTINI Estimator, Procore Estimating, Ediphi, Buildr, Zebel and HeavyBid Pre-Construction. All of them focus on cost, bidding and process, not spatial design.

Search snippets say it promotes DESTINI Cloud, which brings estimating, takeoff, bidding, analytics and collaboration into one place, with standardized templates, version history and AI-generated insights that a person reviews. — [Beck Technology – For Preconstruction Managers & VPs](https://www.beck-technology.com/precon-manager) (snippet only; the page could not be fetched)

### Inferences
- "Precon Manager" is most likely a job title ("preconstruction manager"), or shorthand for Beck Technology's persona page and DESTINI product line, rather than a distinct product. If the user means a specific tool, Beck Technology DESTINI and ConCntric are the most likely candidates. An in-house tool or a small regional product with that name cannot be ruled out.
- Every candidate belongs to the cost, bid and process side of preconstruction: estimating, takeoff, bid leveling, budget-versus-design tracking and scheduling.

### Gaps
- I could not read the vendor pages for Beck Technology and ConCntric directly, so exact current feature lists, 2026 releases and AI features are unconfirmed.
- I could not confirm whether DESTINI's BIM 360 integration has moved to Autodesk Construction Cloud (ACC) or Autodesk Docs, or whether it now reads Revit directly. Search sources named no direct Revit plugin.
- No pricing data was gathered.

## Does it support layout optimization, travel-distance analysis or simulation? What integrations could connect it to such analysis?

### Takeaway
No. None of the "Precon Manager" candidates (DESTINI, ConCntric, PreconSuite, Precon Playbook and the others) advertise floor-plan optimization, path or travel-distance analysis, or operational simulation. The closest feature is cost comparison of design options (DESTINI's "Scenario and Alternate Analysis" and ConCntric's design-cost alignment). That means they could put a price on layout alternatives produced elsewhere. They cannot generate or score layouts for nurse walking.

### Cited Findings
- DESTINI's design-option capability is cost-oriented: "Scenario and Alternate Analysis" for quick cost comparisons of design options. — [Capterra – DESTINI Estimator](https://www.capterra.com/p/161842/DESTINI-Estimator/)
- DESTINI's BIM link runs through Autodesk BIM 360 (3D takeoff and model filtering by estimating properties) and Procore (2D takeoff, financials). These are quantity and cost links, not spatial-analysis links. — [Beck Technology – integrations](https://www.beck-technology.com/destini-estimator-integrations) (snippet)
- One DESTINI user warns that early in design "I can't just rely on BIM models. My estimate depends on the quality of the BIM model." Another said an integration "hasn't been very helpful". — [G2 – DESTINI Estimator reviews](https://www.g2.com/products/destini-estimator/reviews) (as summarized in search results)
- ConCntric is described as covering early estimates, design-cost alignment and scheduling. No layout or simulation feature appeared in any result. — [ConCntric](https://concntric.com/generalcontractors/) (snippet)

### Inferences
- A realistic workflow: (1) generate or analyze unit layouts in a design or simulation tool (Revit with Dynamo Generative Design, Hypar, space-syntax tools, or MedModel, FlexSim or AnyLogic); (2) carry the shortlisted options as Revit models in BIM 360 or ACC; (3) price each option in a precon platform such as DESTINI (BIM 360 takeoff plus Scenario/Alternate Analysis) or ConCntric (design-cost alignment). The precon tool's role is to weigh cost against the operational benefit (walking distance or staff time) that another tool calculates.
- Any travel-distance metric would have to be entered by hand, for example as a custom property or alternate note. No precon tool found has a native field or analysis for it.

### Gaps
- I found no evidence of any precon vendor integrating with simulation tools such as AnyLogic, FlexSim or MedModel, or with generative-design tools such as Refinery, Hypar or TestFit.

## Which software tools are used for hospital unit layout optimization and nurse walking-distance analysis, and what can they do?

### Takeaway
Nurse-travel work uses three families of tools: (a) BIM-native or computational generative design (Revit Generative Design/Dynamo, formerly Refinery; Hypar; custom optimization in research); (b) space syntax and visibility analysis (depthmapX, and Revit/Dynamo plug-ins such as AVA); and (c) discrete-event or agent-based operational simulation (MedModel, FlexSim Healthcare, AnyLogic, plus custom research simulators). Documented healthcare results include a 576 ft per 12-hour shift cut in medication-related walking (MedModel), 15% (agent-based simulation), 26.8% per RN (CADRE simulation) and "walking distances reduced in half" (HKS/ProMedica).

### Cited Findings
**Generative design / computational layout**
- Revit's Generative Design (the successor to Project Refinery) uses a Dynamo graph as the engine of each study. It is not available in Revit LT. — [Autodesk Revit Help – Generative Design](https://help.autodesk.com/cloudhelp/2026/ENU/Revit-GDiR/files/GUID-A1C08759-9B0E-4985-AB47-F3D0AB28C46F.htm); [Revit "Have you tried" – Generative Design](https://help.autodesk.com/cloudhelp/2026/ENU/Revit-HaveYouTried/files/GUID-A2EC3302-CB0E-4648-A3A5-6EE0119119CD.htm)
- An Autodesk University case: ENGIE Axima / AP-HP used Dynamo and Refinery to reorganize a roughly 1,300 m² hospital pharmacy (Saint-Louis Hospital) under constraints on material and people flows, and generated 3D BIM models from the generative space plan. The presenters said the over-constrained site made it a poor first generative-design project. — [AU 2020 – Generative Design Complex Buildings: Optimizing Spaces and Flows](https://www.autodesk.com/autodesk-university/article/Generative-Design-Complex-Buildings-Optimizing-Spaces-and-Flows-Dynamo-and-Refinery-2020); [AU 2019 class](https://www.autodesk.com/autodesk-university/class/Generative-design-Hospital-Pharmacy-optimizing-spacesflows-DynamoRefinery-2019) (snippets)
- Autodesk Research's integrated spatial-structural optimization (ISSO) was built in Dynamo and cites minimizing nurse travel time as an objective. — [Autodesk Research – ISSO paper](https://research.autodesk.com/app/uploads/2023/03/integrated-spatialstructural-optimization-in.pdf_rectdTLT6t6SS7LMw.pdf) (snippet)
- Hypar 2.0 (late 2024) shifted its cloud tool toward space planning. It partnered with Forsight Digital, a healthcare space-planning tool used on more than 14 million sq ft of healthcare space. Department blocks can be arranged by hand or distributed automatically into an existing building, and room-level auto-placement was described as upcoming. Hypar is described as Revit-compatible. — [Hypar blog – healthcare design](https://blog.hypar.io/healthcare-design-with-fastplan-and-hypar-space); [Hypar blog – healthcare pilot](https://blog.hypar.io/healthcare-pilot); [Hypar docs – space planning](https://docs.hypar.io/space-planning); [AEC Magazine – Hypar 2.0](https://aecmag.com/bim/hypar-2-0/)
- Research approaches: TU Delft framed hospital layout planning as mixed-integer programming solved with operations-research solvers — [TU Delft – Optimal Design of new Hospitals](https://repository.tudelft.nl/record/uuid:393e31ad-43f4-4863-ace6-63d7f3d7c97d). Penn State generated optimal and near-optimal department layouts with graph theory and a distance-weighted adjacency score — [Penn State](https://pure.psu.edu/en/publications/evaluating-generated-layouts-in-a-healthcare-departmental-adjacen/). An automatic hospital layout generator using adjacency and infection-risk constraints was limited to rectangular forms — [ECAM 2022](https://emerald.com/insight/content/doi/10.1108/ECAM-08-2022-0757).
- Gensler has published a "Test, Don't Guess" approach for healthcare design (details not verified). — [Gensler blog](https://www.gensler.com/blog/test-dont-guess-a-new-approach-for-healthcare-design)

**Space syntax / visibility**
- depthmapX visibility-graph analysis has been used to benchmark ward layouts. Pachilova and Sailer's "Spaces for Communication Index" (RIBA President's Award for Research) is one example. — [UCL – Pachilova & Sailer 2020](https://discovery.ucl.ac.uk/10097438/1/Pachilova_Sailer_2020_ProvidingCareQualitybyDesign_JOA_published.pdf); [RIBAJ](https://ribaj.com/intelligence/president-s-award-for-research-building-in-quality-providing-care-quality-by-design)
- A 2025 study built space syntax into Revit and Dynamo with custom Python and the AVA package. It computes step depth, metric distance and connectivity for emergency department layouts from patient and nurse viewpoints, and was validated against depthmapX. It notes that most healthcare space syntax work runs in standalone tools outside BIM. — [MDPI Buildings 15(21):3818](https://www.mdpi.com/2075-5309/15/21/3818); [Cardiff ORCA PDF](https://orca.cardiff.ac.uk/id/eprint/181849/1/buildings-15-03818-v4.pdf)
- Visibility and walking pull against each other. In a Korean comparison, an I-shaped nurse station "provides panoramic view... but very rigorous walking". — [koreascience](https://koreascience.kr/article/JAKO201515337344118.pdf); see also [PMC – ICU spatial networks](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4287700)

**Operational simulation**
- MedModel (Texas Tech, D. Pati) compared a design with nurse servers against one with a central medication room. Nurse servers cut medication-related walking by 576 ft per 12-hour shift, and Houston Medical Center adopted that design for its new bed tower. — [CADRE – A MedModel decision support study](https://cadreresearch.org/projects/a-medmodel-decision-support-study)
- A CADRE simulation calculates walking distance automatically for any floor layout. In it, a decentralized model cut estimated total walking per RN by 26.8%. — [CADRE – Towards optimizing inpatient unit design](https://www.cadreresearch.org/projects/towards-optimizing-inpatient-unit-design-and-operation)
- An agent-based simulation of 8 med-surg layouts (Lee, Lee & Shepley 2020) found that a central nurse station and multi-bed rooms placed nearer to it cut simulated nurse walking by 15%. The study was limited to one scenario and did not model acuity or nurse experience. The software was not named in the sources reviewed. — [CHD summary](https://healthdesign.org/node/54290); [Lee 2020 PDF](https://healthdesign.org/sites/default/files/article-pdfs/Lee-2020-CHD-KPS.pdf)
- HKS/ProMedica ran simulation after each design iteration (visibility and walking). The final plan halved walking distances despite a larger floor plate, though results were not yet validated when reported. — [Healthcare Design – "Put to the Test"](https://healthcaredesignmagazine.com/trends/put-test/14436/) (snippet)
- FlexSim HC (FlexSim Healthcare) is built specifically for healthcare facility problems such as patient flow. — [HealthySimulation](https://www.healthysimulation.com/ja/flexsim-healthcare-%E3%81%AF%E6%82%A3%E8%80%85%E3%83%95%E3%83%AD%E3%83%BC%E3%81%AE%E3%82%B7%E3%83%9F%E3%83%A5%E3%83%AC%E3%83%BC%E3%82%B7%E3%83%A7%E3%83%B3%E3%81%A7%E3%83%91%E3%83%95%E3%82%A9%E3%83%BC%E3%83%9E%E3%83%B3%E3%82%B9%E3%82%92%E5%90%91%E4%B8%8A/); [FlexSim HC 5.0 manual](https://es.scribd.com/document/342330395/FlexSimHC-5-0-UserManual-pdf)
- AnyLogic's Pedestrian Library simulates movement in continuous space around walls and other agents, and reports density and length-of-stay statistics. — [AnyLogic 9 help](https://anylogic.help/9/libraries/pedestrian/index.html). It was used to find congested zones and test layout changes at Chile's Barros Luco Hospital — [WSC 2016](https://www.informs-sim.org/wsc16papers/113.pdf) — and for clinic spatial arrangements — [NDSU thesis](https://library.ndsu.edu/ir/handle/10365/31301).
- Background on nurse walking: [Cuningham – How much walking is too much walking](https://cuningham.com/2020/03/30/how-much-walking-too-much-walking); [Cornell – new tool for nursing unit design](https://news.cornell.edu/index%2ephp/node/277658)

### Inferences
- A typical stack: generate candidate layouts in Revit Generative Design/Dynamo or Hypar; score them for distance and visibility with space syntax (depthmapX or AVA in Dynamo) or shortest-path scripts; check operations with MedModel, FlexSim HC or AnyLogic using real task-frequency data; then price the finalists in a precon platform.
- Static shortest-path or space-syntax metrics are cheap and can sit inside a generative loop. Discrete-event or agent-based simulation captures task frequency, acuity and staffing but costs more to run, so it suits validating a shortlist.

### Gaps
- TestFit, Finch (Finch3D) and Autodesk Forma: no search results tied them to healthcare or nurse-travel analysis. TestFit and Finch are generally known for multifamily, parking and residential test-fits, but that is unverified here. Do not claim healthcare capabilities for them.
- Simio and Arena were not researched. They are general discrete-event simulation tools with no healthcare-layout case found in this pass.
- The vendor running MedModel (now part of ProModel) and the current status of the FlexSim HC product line in 2026 were not verified.
- The Autodesk University and Healthcare Design articles were read only through snippets. Full metrics and methods were not verified.
